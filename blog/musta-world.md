Musta is a project with two main goals:
1. an online diary / journal, and
2. a way to share specific entries with specific people I trust.

Pen-and-paper and a dozen different apps are available for the first goal. I've tried some of them but nothing really felt right. Which is why I created [Papel](https://github.com/marvinisaac/papel), one of the predecessors to Musta.

Social networking websites of the early 2000's used to be enough for the second goal, but the social media of today have stopped caring about many things. Privacy and real connections are just some examples.

That's why I'm building Musta. And I'm building it in public moving forward.

I've recently started creating [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@AkoSiMarvin) videos and these blogs will eventually become videos where I talk about Musta.

This is the start of a very challenging journey full of lessons (hopefully 🤞).
